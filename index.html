Company brief

Answer ten questions once. Get one brief you can give to any AI assistant, so its answers sound like your company and stay within your rules.

Your privacy. This page does not send anything anywhere and does not save anything: when you close it, your answers are gone. Download the brief if you want to keep it. When you paste the brief into an assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Mistral, LibreChat or another), that assistant's provider receives it, so only include what you are comfortable sharing with them.

Your brief

How to use it

  1. Open your assistant's settings and look for custom instructions, a project, or a "knowledge" area. The name and the length limit differ between assistants and change over time; check yours.
  2. Paste the brief there. If your assistant has no such place, paste it at the start of a new chat.
  3. Read what the assistant writes before you send it to anyone. The brief helps; it does not make the answers correct.
