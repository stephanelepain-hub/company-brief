# Company brief

Write one brief about your company, once, and give it to any AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Mistral, LibreChat or another.

**Try it:** https://stephanelepain-hub.github.io/company-brief/

You answer ten questions: what you do, who your customers are, what you offer, your prices, your terms, your tone, your rules and what must never be shared. You get one brief to copy or download, and three lines on where to paste it.

- One HTML file. No install, no account, no API key.
- Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved. When you close the page, your answers are gone. The online version is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees that the page was visited, not what you type. Prefer it fully offline? Download `index.html` and open it in your browser.
- When you paste the brief into an assistant, that assistant's provider receives it. Only include what you are happy to share with them.
- MIT licence: any company can use, change and reuse the code.

Status: early version. Tell us what is missing or unclear: open an issue.
